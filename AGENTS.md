# Sanjay Rithik Hospital — laser hair reduction landing page

Variant of the main skin landing page (repo `true-skin-tale`) for laser hair reduction:
"Tired of waxing, shaving, or threading again and again?". Same stack, Dockerfile, Coolify
setup and Twenty CRM endpoint. Leads are labelled "Sanjay Rithik laser hair reduction landing
page" with `landing_page_identifier: "laser-hair-reduction-karur"`.

Copy rules: no shaming of body hair, no invented results, prices, visit counts or policies,
no "permanent", "painless", "no more waxing" or "no downtime". Skin answers never go to analytics.
